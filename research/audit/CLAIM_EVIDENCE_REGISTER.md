## Claim wording that must remain qualified

- “Convergence” must identify the compared state projection and exact tested schedules; RVAR is row-value agreement only.
- “Correctness” must identify the oracle and policy/domain semantics. The additive oracle does not define correct LWW behavior.
- “Efficient,” “lower overhead,” or “superior” requires a fair same-semantics baseline and more evidence than the current two same-seed timing pairs.
- “Safe recovery” must name tested failure boundaries and retain manual RESOLVE review, single-process coordination, and power-loss limitations.
- “Novel” should be reserved until the supplied and authoritative related work is mapped to specific claims; do not infer novelty from feature combinations alone.
